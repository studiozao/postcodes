Who we are

Studio Zao is a London innovation and growth studio. For councils, we are the team that turns a plan into a programme people can use. We design it with local businesses and residents, run it in person, and adapt it as we learn.

We have delivered programmes in Torbay, Tamworth, Tower Hamlets, East Sussex and South London, for councils, partnerships and the people they serve.

How we work

  1. Business-led

    We start from what local businesses and residents need, then design the programme around them.

  2. Local and in person

    Our team is on the ground in the places we work, running sessions and mentoring people directly.

  3. Co-designed

    Residents, businesses and partners shape the programme with us from the start.

  4. Adaptable

    We test, learn and adjust while a programme runs.

  5. AI to make things happen

    We use AI in our own delivery, and help businesses and teams put it to practical use.

  6. Accessible

    We design for people who don't usually take part.

Why the three strands belong together

Councils often fund business support, skills and community engagement from separate budgets and teams. Residents and businesses experience them as one place.

  • Skills→A skills programme can produce new founders.
  • Community→Community engagement shows which support people will actually use.
  • Business→Business support creates the jobs that skills programmes prepare people for.

We work across all three, so whichever strand you start with, we design it to connect to the others.
